# CLA confirmation data

Notice version: 1.0.

This notice applies to the online confirmation process described in [CLA.md](CLA.md).
Confirmations open only after the enabled configuration is merged into `main` and
[maredengb](https://github.com/maredengb) personally posts the matching activation
announcement in [the confirmation thread](https://github.com/maredengb/coffered/issues/2).
Do not post a confirmation before that announcement. It identifies the exact
agreement and notice versions, permanent links and SHA256 hashes.

## Who handles the records

The Recipient defined in [CLA.md](CLA.md), publicly identified by GitHub account
[maredengb](https://github.com/maredengb), handles the Project's confirmation
records. GitHub operates its own account, comment and automation services under
its own terms and privacy statement. The confirmation process does not use an
additional hosted CLA service, identity-verification provider or external signing
database.

## What the confirmation process records

- Public GitHub login and numeric user ID, and the relevant actor type.
- Exact affirmative comment, comment/thread IDs, URLs, platform creation/update
  times, agreement/notice version and SHA256, and first covered PR and commit.
- Contribution references, later check results and limited evidence of their
  provenance. Copies of the agreement, notice and confirmation are retained.

These records can be personal information. No separate legal name, private email,
telephone number, address, identity-document copy or handwritten signature is
requested for the standard process. GitHub account profiles and Git commits may
already contain other information; the checker should not copy unnecessary profile
fields or commit-author email addresses into the confirmation record. Technical
service logs may contain additional data under GitHub's own policies.

## Visibility and choice

The confirmation thread is public. Before submitting a confirmation, You choose
to publish the acceptance comment and its account attribution, understanding that
other people may read and copy them. The confirmation wording includes a separate
explicit acknowledgment of this visibility. The Project also publishes the
minimal CLA check outcome needed for the PR. It does not publish the owner's
private identity record or any exceptional private authorization evidence.

If You do not want a public confirmation, do not post one. Ask for a confidential
alternative without including private material in a public issue. No alternative
is represented as already operating; the affected Contribution waits while an
appropriate process is agreed. A general contact request need not state that You
have or have not signed. No one is required to contribute.

## Purpose, location and retention

The records are used to establish the agreed licensing scope, check subsequent
Contributions, resolve rights questions and preserve necessary evidence. GitHub
hosts and processes the public confirmation and workflow data; the Project does
not promise mainland-China-only storage. The owner keeps access-restricted
backup copies outside the public source tree and public build artifacts. Private
records and backups remain under the owner's control; they are not supplied to a
new signing service by this process. Where applicable law requires further
notice, consent or other cross-border/data-processing steps, those must be
completed before the affected processing is enabled.

Necessary license evidence is kept while the Project relies on the grants and for
any additional period reasonably required by applicable law or an actual dispute.
The owner reviews retention necessity at least annually and removes unrelated
or unnecessary data. No blanket promise of permanent retention or immediate
erasure of still-needed legal evidence is made. The Project cannot erase copies
already made independently by GitHub or other public readers.

## Questions and rights requests

Use the [Project issues](https://github.com/maredengb/coffered/issues) to request a
confidential contact route, without posting private information. The owner handles
appropriate access, correction, deletion and other lawful requests, explaining
any lawful need to retain limited evidence. Identity verification, if necessary
for a particular rights request or dispute, is handled case by case and is not a
routine prerequisite for every contribution. It must not require public disclosure.

A successor must meet the agreement's conditions; contractual succession alone
does not authorize transfer of personal information. Applicable disclosures,
consents and safeguards must be handled separately. This notice does not certify
compliance or signature validity.
